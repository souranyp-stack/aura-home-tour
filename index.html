01The residence

A house built from the headland it stands on.

AURA is a concept for a single-family house on a limestone promontory 38 metres above an open western sea. It has two levels. Below is a stone plinth set into the rock for sleeping and bathing. Above is a glass pavilion for living, cooking and watching the weather come in.

The stone dug out for the plinth becomes its walls. A limestone blade 26 metres long anchors the house to the cliff and carries the roof, which reaches nine metres out over the terrace without a column on the sea side.

03Materials

Six materials, chosen to weather well.

Each sample below is the same surface the film uses, lit in real time. Nothing is painted, laminated or veneered.

  • Split-face limestone

    Quarried from the plinth excavation and dry-laid in long, low courses. Used for the spine, the fin and the hearth.

  • Vein-cut travertine

    Honed and unfilled, so the pores stay open and grip wet feet. Used for the terraces, the pool coping and the gallery wall.

  • White oak

    Wide boards with a hard-wax oil finish. Used for the pavilion floor and the soffit, so floor and ceiling read as one material.

  • Patinated bronze

    Left to darken in the salt air. Used for the roof edge, the mullions and the door pulls.

  • Wool bouclé

    A heavy undyed weave for the living-room seating.

  • Saddle leather

    Vegetable-tanned, used for the lounge chairs. It darkens where hands rest.

05How it lives

Designed for the climate, not against it.

  1. 11 h

    Thermal lag

    Stone walls 600 mm thick soak up the afternoon heat and give it back after midnight.

  2. 0

    Air-conditioned rooms

    The pavilion's glass walls slide open on two sides, so the onshore breeze crosses the room from the sea to the hill.

  3. 86 m²

    Hidden solar

    Photovoltaic panels sit flush behind the bronze roof edge. They can't be seen from the terrace or the sea.

  4. 38 m³

    Rainwater

    A cistern under the entry court collects every winter storm for the garden and the pool.
