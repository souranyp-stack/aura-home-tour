A residence in nine masses

AURA

The living sanctuary

It starts as a sculpture with no doors, no rooms and no address. Keep scrolling and it becomes a home on the edge of the sea.

I

Nine stones
in balance

Before it had a programme, it had proportions. Two blades, three plates, a hearth and a pool. Nothing here is decoration, and every piece will carry weight.

II

Gravity
decides

Each mass falls into its place. The limestone blade drives into the rock. The floor plate reaches nearly five metres past the cliff, and the roof lands last, light as a sheet of paper.

III

Matter rises
from the ground

  • WallsSplit-face limestone, quarried on site
  • FloorsVein-cut travertine & white oak
  • EdgesPatinated bronze
  • OpeningsLow-iron glass, floor to ceiling

IV

Then the world
arrives

A limestone headland, an open western sea and a horizon that belongs to no one.

VI

Inside, the horizon

Oak underfoot, a fire set into the stone, and twenty metres of glass facing the sunset.
